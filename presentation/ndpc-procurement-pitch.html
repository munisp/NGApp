NDSEP: One Platform for Nigeria's Data Sovereignty

What NDSEP Delivers

  • Real-time compliance monitoring across all 12 Nigerian sectors (Banking, Telecom, Oil & Gas, Healthcare, Fintech, etc.)
  • Automated enforcement workflows — from complaint intake to penalty calculation to appeal management
  • DPCO audit management — one-stop shop for accredited Data Protection Compliance Organizations
  • Cross-border transfer oversight — TIA assessments, residency checks, and approval workflows per NDPA Article 28
  • Citizen DSAR portal — data subject access, erasure, portability, and objection requests with SLA tracking
  • Financial enforcement — penalty calculation per NDPA Article 47, with TigerBeetle-powered double-entry ledger

Architecture

  • 18 organizations currently registered across 12 sectors
  • 47 database tables covering compliance, enforcement, DPCO, finance, security
  • 205 UI screens — PWA + React Native + Flutter mobile
  • 12 middleware services — PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka, Temporal, Keycloak, TigerBeetle, OpenSearch, APISIX, Dapr, Fluvio, Permify, Mojaloop
  • Go + Rust + Python workers for ML risk prediction, BGP monitoring, and network DPI
  • Offline-first — service worker + IndexedDB for low-bandwidth environments

Platform Capabilities

Compliance Engine

Real-time policy monitoring, OPA policy editor, violation detection with 100-point scoring (A-F grades), and automated gap analysis

Enforcement Workflow

Case management from intake to resolution. Automated penalty calculation per NDPA Article 47 (2% turnover cap). Appeal tracking with Temporal orchestration.

Penalty Calculator

Business rules engine: severity-based amounts (N500K-N10M), repeat offender multipliers, data volume scaling, turnover cap enforcement. Double-entry ledger.

Cross-Border Transfer Control

Transfer Impact Assessments (TIA), residency checks, approval workflows. Real-time monitoring of data flows across jurisdictions.

Data Catalog & Discovery

Automated asset discovery, data lineage visualization, classification (Tier 1 PII through Tier 5 Public). BGP route monitoring for data sovereignty.

Citizen DSAR Portal

Self-service portal for data subject rights: access, erasure, portability, rectification, restriction, objection. SLA tracking with auto-escalation.

AI Systems Registry

Registration and risk assessment of AI systems per risk level (minimal to unacceptable). Audit scheduling and cross-border transfer tracking.

Network Deep Packet Inspection

BGP route monitoring for hijack/leak detection, RPKI validation, IXP tracking. Real-time sovereignty enforcement at the network level.

DPCO Integration — One-Stop Audit Shop

Accredited Data Protection Compliance Organizations manage the entire audit lifecycle on NDSEP

DPCO Workflow

  • DPCO Registration & Licensing — Starter, Professional, Enterprise tiers with subscription management
  • Client Onboarding — Risk-tiered clients (Low to Critical) with compliance scoring
  • 8-Stage Audit Pipeline — Initiated → Data Mapping → Gap Assessment → Fieldwork → Findings Review → Management Response → Report Issued → CAR Filed
  • Evidence Vault — Secure document management with SHA-256 hashing and tamper detection
  • Control Ratings — Per-article NDPA compliance ratings (Compliant, Partial, Non-Compliant, N/A)
  • Invoice & Payment — Automated invoicing, VAT calculation, platform fee splits, revenue reconciliation

Security Posture — Enterprise Grade

DDoS Protection

Rate limiting (100 req/15min per IP), IP-based circuit breaker, progressive blocking. Protects all API endpoints.

Ransomware Defense

File integrity monitoring, hash-chained audit log (tamper-evident), canary file detection, bulk delete alerts.

Policy-Based Access Control (PBAC)

Powered by Permify. Role-based permissions with fine-grained policy enforcement. Admin, Auditor, DPO, Citizen roles.

Security Headers

CSP (default-src 'self'), HSTS, X-Frame-Options: DENY, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Request-ID tracking.

Offline Resilience

Service worker with IndexedDB queue. Adaptive bandwidth detection. Designed for low-connectivity environments in rural Nigeria.

Audit Trail

Hash-chained, append-only audit log. Every data access, modification, and enforcement action recorded with tamper detection.
