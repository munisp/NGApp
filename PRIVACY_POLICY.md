# Privacy Policy for African Fintech Mobile App

**Last Updated**: January 22, 2026

## Introduction

African Fintech ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
- Register for an account
- Complete KYC (Know Your Customer) verification
- Make transactions
- Contact customer support

**Personal information may include**:
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Date of birth
- Physical address
- Government-issued ID documents (passport, driver's license, national ID)
- Facial recognition data (for identity verification)
- Bank account information
- Transaction history
- Financial data (income, expenses, budgets)

### Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain information, including:
- Device information (model, operating system, unique device identifiers)
- IP address
- Location data (with your permission)
- App usage data and analytics
- Log files and crash reports

### Financial Information

We collect and process financial information including:
- Payment card details (encrypted and tokenized)
- Bank account numbers
- Transaction amounts and descriptions
- Investment portfolio data
- Cryptocurrency wallet addresses

### Biometric Data

With your explicit consent, we collect biometric data for authentication purposes:
- Fingerprint data (stored locally on your device)
- Facial recognition data (for KYC verification)

**Note**: Biometric data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

### Provide Services
- Create and manage your account
- Process payments and transactions
- Verify your identity (KYC compliance)
- Provide customer support
- Send transaction notifications

### Improve Services
- Analyze app usage and performance
- Develop new features
- Personalize your experience
- Provide AI-powered financial recommendations

### Security and Fraud Prevention
- Detect and prevent fraudulent transactions
- Monitor for suspicious activity
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
- Protect against security threats

### Marketing and Communications
- Send promotional offers (with your consent)
- Provide financial education content
- Notify you of new features
- Conduct surveys and research

We may share your information with:

### Service Providers
- Payment processors (Paystack, Flutterwave)

### Financial Institutions
- Banks and financial institutions (for transactions)

- Law enforcement agencies (when required by law)

### Business Transfers
- In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

### Technical Safeguards
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
- Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology
- Hardware Security Module (HSM) integration
- Biometric authentication
- Multi-factor authentication
- Regular security audits

### Organizational Safeguards
- Employee training on data protection
- Access controls and authorization
- Incident response procedures
- Regular security assessments

### Payment Security
- PCI DSS compliance
- Tokenization of payment card data
- Secure payment gateway integration

## Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

### Access and Portability
- Request a copy of your personal data
- Export your transaction history
- Download your financial reports

### Correction
- Update your personal information
- Correct inaccurate data

### Deletion
- Request deletion of your account
- Remove specific data (subject to legal requirements)

### Opt-Out
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails
- Disable push notifications
- Revoke location permissions

### Data Retention
- We retain your data for as long as your account is active
- After account deletion, we retain data for 7 years (regulatory requirement)
- Transaction records are retained for tax and audit purposes

## Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.
